## What this person would champion

Jamie would be excited by projects that make AI systems legible — interpretability tools, accountability frameworks, and participatory methods that give citizens and government users real understanding of what AI is doing. He'd champion open-source safety infrastructure that government departments can actually use, and projects that take systemic risk seriously by design.

## What this person would discount

He'd be sceptical of AI projects that claim public benefit but have no interpretability or accountability layer — black boxes deployed on government data without transparency mechanisms. He'd also discount "participatory AI" projects that use the language of participation but lack genuine mechanisms for community input.
