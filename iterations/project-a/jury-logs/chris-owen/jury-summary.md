## Interpretation

The unanimous result reflects a strong values signal: when the system prompt included the Xinhua quote about "being there at the moment when they realise they can do it," all five models recognised Humble Data Workshop as the direct analogue of Social Hackers Academy and CodeYourFuture applied to data science for excluded groups. The convergence is unusually clean — no model, across any of three runs, chose a different project.

The pedagogical specificity of the values profile (volunteer-run, free, open-source curriculum, excluded groups, conference-piggybacking replication model) was a strong enough discriminator to produce clean consensus even across models with different training contexts and RLHF lineages.
