## Analysis

The unanimous result (15/15) reflects the unusual clarity of Martina Orlea's stated values. Her explicit Newspeak House interest in "analysing/fighting information warfare" — combined with her direct experience in Romania's 2025 presidential election cycle — creates a very tight match with DISARM Frameworks, which is specifically the professional toolkit for exactly that work. No other project on the shortlist addresses information warfare as directly, at scale, and with the institutional adoption that validates its practical effectiveness.

The second-place candidate — Who Targets Me Trends — was never selected by any model, suggesting the jury read Martina's values as oriented toward understanding the *tactics* of information warfare (DISARM's domain: mapping TTPs systematically) rather than *monitoring* political advertising (Who Targets Me's domain). This is a plausible inference, though a human reviewer might question whether the distinction holds: Martina's campaign work also involved programmatic advertising strategy.

The strong consensus could also reflect a genuine constraint: the shortlist of 20 contained several strong candidates for disinformation/campaign tech, but DISARM was the only one explicitly designed as a counter-influence operations framework. Had the shortlist included different candidates, the votes might have been more distributed.
