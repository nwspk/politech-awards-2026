## Interpretation

The 11–4 split reflects a genuine values tension within Fatima's profile:

**The Decidim case (majority):** GPT-4o, Mistral, Llama, and 2/3 Claude runs all converge on Decidim as the cleanest expression of open source civic commons — genuinely free/libre, cooperative governance, hundreds of government deployments, developer accessible. It is the maximally open, maximally civic infrastructure choice, and the breadth of deployment means it has concrete democratic impact rather than proof-of-concept status.

**The Polis case (minority):** Claude's third run and Gemini's consistent choice. Polis sits at the precise intersection of "AI governance as active research" and "open source civic infrastructure" — it is simultaneously a tool that uses AI and a demonstration of how AI-powered tools can be governed publicly. For models that weighted Fatima's Newspeak House AI governance research focus most heavily, Polis felt like the more personally resonant pick: less about scale and more about the research question she is actually working on.

**The underlying tension:** Decidim is a larger, more mature, more deployed civic infrastructure — the "production" answer. Polis is smaller but occupies the AI × governance × open source intersection that defines Fatima's current intellectual project — the "research" answer. This is a genuine values split, not noise.
