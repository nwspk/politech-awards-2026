## v5 interpretation notes

**Why C6 jumped from 12 to 30:**
The v2 inference from GitLab blog writing was cautious — the "MEDIUM (max 12 pts)" weight reflected that AI infrastructure governance was a consistent signal but not the loudest one in the evidence base. Fatima's direct feedback corrects this: C6 is now equal weight with C1/C2/C3. This is the biggest single change from v2 to v5, and it substantially reshapes the top rankings (projects with strong community AI governance that were mid-table in v2 now compete at the top).

**Why C5 dropped from 12 to 6:**
In v2, implementation maturity was weighted MEDIUM based on Fatima's practice-oriented career. The direct feedback explicitly demotes it. This reflects an agency-first insight: a project can be widely deployed and still fail the agency test if communities cannot govern it themselves. Scale is a lagging indicator; conditions for self-governance are the leading one.

**M_AGENCY as new modifier:**
The v2 constitution had no equivalent to M_AGENCY. The five existing modifiers covered community ownership (M1), extractive data (M2), underserved design (M3), power-structure digitisation (M4), and inclusive dev community (M5). M_AGENCY is a direct translation of the "forkability + logic exposure + agency over behaviour" cluster in Fatima's direct feedback. It can push high-scoring projects to the maximum and pull platform-dependent tools significantly lower.
