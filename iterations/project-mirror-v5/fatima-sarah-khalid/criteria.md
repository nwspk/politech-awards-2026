## Scoring Guidance

### C1: Accessibility (max 30)
- 25–30: explicitly serves excluded/marginalized/underserved communities; named barriers with specific removal mechanisms
- 15–24: accessibility is a genuine consideration but not the primary design constraint
- 5–14: serves general public, accessibility is incidental
- 0–4: primarily serves professionals, government agencies, or already-engaged experts

### C2: Open source + community governance (max 30)
- 25–30: fully open source, active community governance, forkable architecture with documented self-hosting, community can re-govern locally
- 15–24: open source with limited governance; or good governance but limited forkability
- 5–14: open source but institutionally controlled; or community governance claims without evidence
- 0–4: proprietary; or open source in name only

**v5 key test:** Can the governed community fork and re-deploy this without institutional permission?

### C3: Legibility + Agency (max 30)
- 25–30: makes complex processes legible AND creates conditions for citizen action, shaping, forking, or contesting
- 15–24: makes processes legible with some action pathway; participatory but not fully agency-raising
- 5–14: informational only; legibility without power to act
- 0–4: digital interface to opacity; serves institutions more than citizens

**v5 key test:** Does legibility lead to agency, or is it informational only?

### C4: Inclusive community-building (max 12)
- 9–12: documented structural mechanisms for inclusion; diverse leadership; DEI audits; active mentorship programs
- 5–8: some inclusion practices; welcomes diversity without structural mechanisms
- 1–4: inclusion claimed but not evidenced
- 0: exclusive or homogeneous

### C5: Implementation maturity (max 6) — LOW weight
- 5–6: large-scale deployment; formal government/institutional adoption; active maintenance
- 3–4: real users, real deployment, active development
- 1–2: early deployment or prototype with evidence of use
- 0: abandoned, dead link, no evidence of deployment

### C6: AI/tech as community infrastructure (max 30)
- 25–30: AI/tech explicitly under community governance; logic transparent/auditable; locally deployable; exposes decision-making; OR non-AI project that is federated, privacy-first, decentralised community infrastructure
- 15–24: positive AI/tech governance signals; or neutral non-AI project with some community infrastructure qualities
- 5–14: AI present but governance unclear; or non-AI platform with limited community control
- 0–4: AI as black box; centralises decision power; surveillance; extractive data; OR project that increases institutional power at community expense

**v5 key test:** Can the community govern the AI component, or do they depend on an institution to run it?

### C7: Cross-jurisdictional (max 6) — LOW weight
- 5–6: documented multi-jurisdictional deployment; explicitly designed for replicability
- 3–4: some cross-border evidence; open standards; generalisable methodology
- 1–2: single jurisdiction but transferable in principle
- 0: highly context-specific, no transferability evidence
