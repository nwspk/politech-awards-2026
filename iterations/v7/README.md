## Quick summary

- This record is grounded only in Davit's own writing and his manual ranking sheet.
- Agent-generated shortlist/winner estimations from the PR are intentionally excluded.
- Current top-ranked project in Davit's sheet is `Diia` at 91%.

## Heuristic

Manual scoring by Davit using eight criteria (columns in the ranking sheet):

- Real-world political relevance
- Movement usefulness
- Track record
- Generalizability
- Evidence quality
- Systemic significance
- Timeliness
- Integrity over hype

Each project is scored in percentages per criterion plus an `Overall` percentage.

## Rationale

From Davit's email (Mar 22, 2026):

- "the AI leader is my number two choice, and my top choice is Diia"
- Diia rationale he wrote: one-country deployment but transferable infrastructure in principle, 20M+ users, and practical wartime value through digital passport access
- He also noted familiarity effects in his own review
- He additionally highlighted LiquidFeedback as a project he knows and sees as experimentally strong with field use
