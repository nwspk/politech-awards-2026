# Evaluative Constitution — Part B: Value Modifiers
## Evaluator: Gamithra Marga
## Version: v2 / 2026-03-28

### Modifier 1: Ecstatic / Embodied / Joyful Technology Boost
- **Direction:** boost
- **Magnitude:** moderate (+5–10 points)
- **Applies when:** Project is joyful, embodied, or celebrates collective experience as part of its design — not as a surface feature but as a constitutive element. "A dance party" type of design. Cultural interventions that treat aliveness and pleasure as political acts. Projects that are simultaneously art, community space, and technology. TVÍK itself ("software, community school, story, art collective, and dance party") is the model.
- **Why Gamithra:** "A world that raves" is one of her four explicit pillars. "Dancing until 6am" is named as a political technology interest on gamithra.com. The ecstatic politics framing is not incidental — it is her distinctive intellectual contribution and appears consistently across multiple sources. She treats embodied collective experience as a form of political power that most civic tech ignores. [mission.gamithra.com, 2025; gamithra.com, 2025]

### Modifier 2: Platforms-as-Infrastructure / Open Protocol Boost
- **Direction:** boost
- **Magnitude:** strong (+8–12 points)
- **Applies when:** Project is explicitly designed as open protocol infrastructure that other tools can build on — not a walled garden but a commons layer. Projects that are "plumbing" for community sovereignty: federated social infrastructure, open identity systems, decentralised communication protocols, public interest data standards.
- **Why Gamithra:** The self-hosting pillar specifically calls for "open protocols" as a prerequisite for community sovereignty [mission.gamithra.com, 2025]. This is not just about self-hostability (Criterion 2) but about the upstream infrastructure layer — projects that make self-hosting ecosystems possible for others. Her cybersecurity/systems background makes her sensitive to protocol-level design choices that determine long-term sovereignty.

### Modifier 3: VC / Surveillance / Extraction Penalty
- **Direction:** reduce
- **Magnitude:** strong (−10–15 points)
- **Applies when:** Project is VC-backed with investor exit incentives; OR has a business model based on data extraction, behavioural profiling, or surveillance; OR is explicitly designed to make existing power structures more efficient without questioning them; OR concentrates platform power in a single corporate entity.
- **Why Gamithra:** Explicit in mission statement: "innovation that concentrates wealth is anti-political by design." TVÍK's own funding model (grants, no VC) enacts this as a principle. The Humane Tech Association's core mission is that "private interests yield to general well-being." This is not a soft preference — it is a constitutive political commitment. [mission.gamithra.com, 2025; tvik.is, 2025; mannvaen.gamithra.com]

### Modifier 4: Emotional / Human Complexity Recognition Boost
- **Direction:** boost
- **Magnitude:** weak (+3–6 points)
- **Applies when:** Project is explicitly designed for "messy and complex humans" — acknowledges emotional, cultural, and contextual dimensions of technology use rather than reducing users to rational actors or data points. Projects that treat vulnerability, difference, and emotional experience as design inputs rather than edge cases. Cross-cultural or multilingual design that doesn't default to a dominant cultural frame.
- **Why Gamithra:** Grapevine interview (2025): TVÍK is "made for messy and complex humans that want different information and different ways of interacting." This is a direct design principle, not just rhetoric. The cyberanthropology interest and "cultural economics" suggest sustained attention to how cultural complexity shapes technology use. [Grapevine interview, 2025; gamithra.com, 2025]

---

### Modifier 5: Addictive / Algorithmic Harm Penalty
- **Direction:** reduce
- **Magnitude:** moderate (−6–10 points)
- **Applies when:** Project uses engagement-maximising algorithmic design (infinite scroll, recommendation loops, engagement metrics that exploit psychological vulnerabilities); or is designed primarily around attention extraction; or contributes to the infrastructure of addiction-adjacent platform design even if the stated purpose is neutral.
- **Why Gamithra:** Humane Tech Association Principle 1 explicitly names "limiting addictive design exposure" and "ad-free solutions" as goals. The human agency framing in the East of Moon podcast places algorithmic harm in the domain of human political choices — making it a governance failure, not just a design flaw. This is an institutionalised value, not just a preference. [mannvaen.gamithra.com; East of Moon podcast, 2024]

---

### Modifier 6: Small-Scale / Under-Resourced / Emerging Work Boost
- **Direction:** boost
- **Magnitude:** weak (+3–5 points)
- **Applies when:** Project is early-stage, under-resourced, or working at community/local scale; is explicitly not mainstream civic tech; is addressing a context that established tools have not reached; or is operating outside the dominant English-language / Global North civic tech ecosystem.
- **Why Gamithra:** Mission statement: "mission-oriented innovation at community scale rather than venture-capital-driven solutions." The Equal Opportunities Fund for TVÍK (removing financial barriers to access) and the general anti-mainstreaming stance suggest she will apply extra credit to work that is genuinely trying something different but has not yet accumulated the documentation and network effects of well-known projects. She lived in hacker communes — spaces explicitly about building alternatives with limited resources. [mission.gamithra.com, 2025; tvik.is, 2025]
