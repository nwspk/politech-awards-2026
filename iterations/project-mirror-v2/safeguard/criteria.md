### Weight distribution rationale

The three HIGH-weight criteria (C1, C2, C3) at 20 points each reflect Safeguard's three most evidenced commitments: enforcement infrastructure, participatory governance, and lifecycle governance. These three themes appear across every major source.

The three MEDIUM-weight criteria (C4, C5, C6) at 12 points each reflect important but secondary priorities: institutional infrastructure, cross-jurisdictional scope, and evidence rigour. These are consistent with his background but less directly articulated as evaluative priorities.

C7 (compute governance) at 6 points reflects a real but weakly evidenced priority. The move to Lucid Computing is the strongest signal, but absent any authored content from Safeguard about compute governance, this criterion is kept at minimal weight.

Total raw weight: 20+20+20+12+12+12+6 = 102. Criteria scores are computed as the sum of per-criterion scores (each scored 0 to the criterion weight), giving a criteria total out of 102, then used directly (not normalised to 100).
