## Evidence Summary

**What is confirmed.** Huda Abdirahim is a 2025/26 Newspeak House Fellowship candidate. This is the most firmly anchored fact: her name appears on the official cohort listing page alongside the other 17 candidates whose profiles are being researched in this pipeline. She is Kenya-based (consistent across LinkedIn and the broader web) and educated at the University of Mount Kenya (from LinkedIn snippet). She has a presence on Foundance, a platform designed for startup co-founders to find collaborators, which is weakly consistent with the bio's claim that she co-founded TreasureCorp.

**What the bio asserts but cannot be confirmed externally.** The bio describes Huda as a software engineer, a co-founder of TreasureCorp (described as a DAO treasury analytics platform), and someone working in digital asset custody. TreasureCorp exists and is publicly accessible at treasurecorp.io — the product is real, the beta programme is live, and the mission (DAO treasury analytics) is well-described. However, TreasureCorp's website, Twitter, and all indexed mentions name no team members whatsoever. There is therefore no public corroboration that Huda Abdirahim specifically co-founded or works at TreasureCorp. This is a gap, not a contradiction. The product is sufficiently niche and early-stage that founder non-disclosure would not be unusual.

**Values signal.** TreasureCorp's mission — making DAO treasury management transparent, analytically rigorous, and accessible — is strongly aligned with the values described in the bio: collective finance, budget transparency, programmable governance, and how code shapes power in decentralised organisations. If Huda is indeed a co-founder, this would make TreasureCorp her primary public-facing artifact. The platform sits at the intersection of civic tech and crypto governance, consistent with Newspeak House's focus on civic technology, and with the bio's framing of DAO governance as political infrastructure.

**What is ambiguous.** The digital asset custody claim is a distinct professional thread from DAO analytics — custody work is typically enterprise/institutional finance, while DAO analytics is community/governance-oriented. These could represent different roles in her career trajectory, or the custody background may be the foundation from which TreasureCorp emerged (understanding how asset control and multi-sig governance actually work in practice). Without a datable CV or LinkedIn content, the sequence cannot be established.

**What is missing.** There is no public writing — no blog posts, Substack, Mirror.xyz articles, or substantive social media threads — attributed to Huda Abdirahim. There are no conference talks, podcast appearances, or academic papers. GitHub shows no profile matching her name with DAO or blockchain relevance. The public record is thin almost entirely because her work appears to be early-stage, pre-announcement, or conducted in non-public channels. This is itself meaningful: she is a practitioner building tools rather than a public intellectual publishing analysis.
