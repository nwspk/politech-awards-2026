### Verified Source List

Sources confirmed as belonging to the correct subject, with accurate claim descriptions:

1. **York Research Database** — Alexandra Ciocanel, Research Associate, University of York, Social Policy and Social Work dept, ORCID 0000-0001-9550-9755. 9 publications 2022–2026. Code Encounters project 2022–2023.

2. **"Automation hesitancy" (IC&S 2024)** — Co-authored with Burrows, Cussens, Wallace, Beer. Develops concept of automation hesitancy: decision-makers in UK PRS are reluctant to fully trust algorithmic processing; only 32% of landlords fully trust it. Human override does not equal accountability — hesitancy is contingent and transitional. Qualitative data from private rental sector professionals.

3. **"Open Banking and data reassurance" (IC&S 2024)** — Co-authored. Open Banking adoption in tenant referencing functions as "data reassurance" (institutional legitimation) rather than tenant empowerment. Expanded profiling under empowerment framing. 50 qualitative interviews with landlords, letting agents, tenants, referencing companies.

4. **"Algorithmic dwelling?" (IC&S 2024)** — Co-authored. Digital technologies as intermediaries in housing access. How algorithmic tools reshape enactment of home.

5. **"Valuing the manual" (Social & Cultural Geography, Nov 2024)** — Co-authored with Beer, Wallace, Burrows, Cussens. Published Social and Cultural Geography 25(10). Three types of manual work (form completion, quality checking, approval) within algorithmic housing systems. Socio-technical arrangements require ongoing human engagement. Visible human involvement can mask structural automation.

6. **"Algorithmic tenancies and the ordinal tenant" (Housing Studies 2025)** — Lead or co-author. Based on 50 interviews and landlord survey. "Ordinal tenant" concept: digital risk profiling ranks tenants producing winners and losers. Specific excluded populations: young people, migrants, adverse-credit histories, precarious employment. Raises accountability and fairness concerns. Uses Fourcade and Healy's "eigencapital" frame.

7. **"Landlords' and tenants' pathways into renting: Romania" (Housing Studies 2025)** — Solo/lead paper. Uses visual-elicitation and life-history interviews. Rental housing as service infrastructure for life-projects.

8. **"Liquid homeownership" (Economic Anthropology 2025)** — Solo paper on Bucharest mortgage borrowers; housing financialization as temporal/class project.

9. **EASA 2026 abstract** — "Translation Work and Categorical Thresholds in Mortgage Credit Applications." Ethnographic fieldwork at Bucharest mortgage brokerage. Brokers navigate between enforcing and subverting algorithm-based classifications. State-defined categories (employment type, marital status, disability) as unstable proxies for creditworthiness. Financial infrastructures exercise bureaucratic authority over housing access without formal government oversight.

10. **Nuffield Foundation Code Encounters project** — Team member, University of York. £212,928 funded, Jan 2022–Oct 2024. 120 interviews total across project. Four output reports published 2024. Ciocanel was symposium organiser (Jan 2023).

11. **HSA blog: "The rise of algorithmic tenant referencing"** — Co-authored. Explicit accountability gap: "no reciprocal data sharing arrangements in the PRS to test model accuracy." Advocates: transparency and regulation of data volumes, equitable access to mainstream rental housing, sensitivity to equality outcomes. Young people, migrants, adverse credit histories named as systematically disadvantaged.
