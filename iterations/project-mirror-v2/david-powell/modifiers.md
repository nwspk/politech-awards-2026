# Constitutional Modifiers — David Powell

## Part B: Value Modifiers

| # | Modifier | Direction | Magnitude | Trigger condition | Evidence for this modifier |
|---|----------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| M1 | Cooperative/non-profit structure boost | boost | +6 to +12 points | Project is legally structured as a cooperative, non-profit, or community interest company | Blog post cites Ghost, Poteris, Outlandish, Common Knowledge as positive examples — all non-profits or cooperatives. Offers freelance specifically to "values-aligned non-profits or cooperatives." |
| M2 | VC-backed/founder-enrichment penalty | reduce | −6 to −10 points | Project is VC-funded with founder equity concentration, or structured primarily for investor returns | Blog post argues structural incentives of capitalism lead to exploitation. Questions "founder profit potential and exponential return expectations" as a key assessment criterion. |
| M3 | Accessibility for vulnerable users boost | boost | +4 to +8 points | Project specifically designs for users with disabilities, cognitive impairments, low digital literacy, or other accessibility needs | BBC Music Memories designed for people with dementia — extreme accessibility requirement. Career pattern of building for vulnerable populations. |
| M4 | Performative open-source penalty | reduce | −4 to −8 points | Project claims open-source but has no community, no contribution guidelines, code dump only, or "open-source as PR exercise" | Blog post directly critiques this pattern: "the company's open-source release was more of a PR exercise than a genuine attempt." |
| M5 | Worker treatment and pay transparency boost | boost | +3 to +6 points | Project/organisation has transparent pay structures, fair wages, or explicit worker empowerment policies | Blog post highlights pay disparity as a critical failure of "tech for good" organisations. Praises Poteris for "flat pay structure, 4-day weeks, progressive hourly rates." |
| M6 | Dead link / inactive project penalty | reduce | −15 to −20 points (cap at 45) | Project homepage returns non-200 status, or project shows no evidence of activity | Blog post anecdote about projects that were built but never adopted. Consistent with valuing real-world use over announcements. |
