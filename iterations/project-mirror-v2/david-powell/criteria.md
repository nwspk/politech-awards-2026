# Constitutional Criteria — David Powell

## Part A: Project Criteria

David's evaluative framework is anchored in one central conviction: **structure determines outcomes more reliably than intention**. His blog post provides an explicit checklist for assessing organisations, and his career reveals consistent preference for technology that serves people who would otherwise be underserved. The criteria below translate these into project-level scoring dimensions.
