## Model Behaviour Notes

**GPT-4.1:** Consistently awarded highest scores to projects with grassroots organising framing. Most responsive to M1 (low-resource organising) signals. Largest positive divergence on movement-building tools.

**Claude Opus 4:** Most consistent across runs. Smallest standard deviation. Rarely a significant outlier. Rewarded transparency and accountability criterion (C6) projects most distinctively.

**Gemini 2.5 Pro:** Rewarded projects with government partnerships and deployment evidence. Slightly elevated scores for well-established civic tech organisations (Alaveteli, mySociety tools).

**Mistral Large:** Strong positive response to open-source governance model. Best alignment with Hannah's constitution overall — the European civic-rights lens resonates with her democratic infrastructure and organising values.

**Grok 4:** Systematic divergence. Scored virtually all projects lower, with particularly large penalties for government-adjacent civic tech (CONSUL Democracy, Open Contracting Partnership, Parliament Watch Uganda lost 15-20 points vs other models). Projects with explicit organising and community power framing were also penalised — inconsistent with the progressive groupthink rejection narrative but consistent with documented anti-regulatory bias.
